Himalayan Women Awareness and Livelihood Society

Founded under the Societies Registration Act 21, 1860, HIMWAL Society is committed to building resilient ecosystems and communities in Uttarakhand.

We work across rural areas and urban centers in the region, operating locally to implement programs that bridge traditional ecological wisdom with modern conservation efforts. From biodiversity assessment to empowering rural women with livelihood opportunities, our approach is holistic and community-driven.

Our Mission

Committed to conserving the environment and safeguarding the unique cultural heritage of Uttarakhand through sustainable action and community engagement.

Our Core Goals

Targeted grassroots action for a resilient Himalayan future.

Health & Nutrition

Empowering rural women through the Bhojan Vikas Karyakram.

Seed Conservation

Preserving genetic resources via Beej Sanrakhshan Karyakram.

Environment Awareness

Engaging youth through Paryavaran Jagrukta Karyakram.

Cultural Heritage

Protecting the legacy of Lok Sanskriti and the traditional Dhol.

Our Growing Impact

Driving measurable change in Himalayan communities through dedicated grassroots work.
