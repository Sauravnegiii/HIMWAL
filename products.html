Community Products

Pure, organic, and handmade by HIMWAL's women artisans in rural Uttarakhand.

Home › Products
🌿 Pure, Handmade & Organic We empower Pahadi women in rural Uttarakhand by providing them with sustainable livelihood opportunities. Every purchase directly supports artisan families and sustains our community initiatives.

Pickles & Chutney Powders

Authentic garlic, mixed vegetable, lemon, and stuffed chilli pickles, alongside instant dried mountain chutney blends (Bhangjeera, Pudina, Sesame).

Pahadi Salt (पिस्यू लूँन)

Traditional rock salt ground with organic seeds and herbs. Available in Hemp seed, Flax seed, Bhangjeera, Garlic/Coriander, Sesame, and Timur flavors.

Himalayan Pulses & Rajma

Pure, chemical-free Chakrata Rajma, Rajma Chhemi, Gehat (Horse gram), Black Gram (Urad), and Yellow/Black Soyabean from high-altitude farms.

Badi, Flours & Ladoos

Handcrafted Urad, Moong, and Mixed Dal Badis, organic millet and wheat flours, roasted Chausa blends, and wholesome Manduwa (Ragi) ladoos.

Herbal Dhoop & Sacred Goods

Hand-rolled organic medicinal herbal dhoop packets, sun-dried apple slices, and pure Gangotri Gangajal sourced directly from the Himalayas.

Handcrafted Souvenirs & Art

Hand-painted keychains and fridge magnets showcasing Uttarakhand heritage, custom canvas mountain paintings, and crochet accessories.

Your Purchase Makes a Difference

Every product you buy is prepared by a woman who has partnered with HIMWAL's livelihood program. The income she earns goes directly to her family, supports her children's education, and strengthens her rural community. By choosing our organic and handmade goods, you are participating in a cycle of lasting change in the Himalayas.
