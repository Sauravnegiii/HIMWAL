Our Core Programs

Grassroots initiatives driving sustainable change, food security, and cultural preservation across Uttarakhand.

Home › Programs

Four Pillars of Our Work

Direct community engagement focusing on nutrition, indigenous seeds, environmental responsibility, and Pahadi heritage.

Bhojan Vikas Karyakram (भोजन विकास कार्यक्रम)

Millet Nutrition & Food Security

We educate rural women in the Joshimath block about the rich nutritional values and health benefits of traditional crops like millets and local pulses[cite: 3]. Through expert-led recipe workshops and community sessions, we actively encourage healthy food habits and local agricultural sustainability[cite: 3].

Beej Sanrakhshan Karyakram (बीज संरक्षण कार्यक्रम)

Traditional Seed Conservation

We collect and preserve indigenous seed varieties from high-altitude Himalayan villages across Uttarakhand.
By safeguarding these genetic resources and distributing them to progressive farmers, we protect agricultural biodiversity and combat climate vulnerability.

Paryavaran Jagrukta Karyakram (पर्यावरण जागरूकता कार्यक्रम)

Environmental Awareness & Education

Inspiring youth and communities through interactive webinars, expert lectures, and student activities (painting, quizzes, essay writing)[cite: 3]. We actively celebrate National Wildlife Week, World Wetlands Day, National Science Day, World Water Day, and Earth Day[cite: 3].

Lok Sanskriti Sanrakshan (लोक संस्कृति संरक्षण)

Cultural Heritage Conservation

Focusing on the Painkhanda (Joshimath) Development Block in Phase I, we document, archive, and promote tangible and intangible Pahadi assets—including folk music, traditional instruments like the Dhol, rituals, and folklore—in partnership with cultural institutions[cite: 3].

Program Impact

Our grassroots work is creating measurable change across Himalayan communities through education, conservation, and livelihoods.
